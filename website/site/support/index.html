Sheprd for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch

Support

The app follows the agents and terminals Sheprd runs on your computers. It needs Sheprd on a Mac, Linux, or Windows computer to pair with; without one, Explore the Demo on its first screen shows how it works.

  1. 1

    Install Sheprd on your computer

    Get it from sheprd.org. It is free.

  2. 2

    Open pairing

    In Sheprd, turn on remote control in Settings and choose Pair device.

  3. 3

    Pair the app

    Tap Pair a Computer and scan the code, or keep the phone nearby to pair over Bluetooth. Compare the six digits, then choose Allow on the computer.

Common questions

The app says a computer is offline.
Sheprd must be running on it. Away from its network, reach it through Tailscale or a Sheprd relay, which Settings > Relays lists.
Notifications do not arrive.
Check that notifications are allowed for Sheprd in iOS Settings, that Settings > Notifications > Push Relay is on in the app, and that the computer shows the phone as ready in Sheprd's remote control settings.
How do I stop a phone from reaching a computer?
Unpair it in the app's Settings > Computers, or revoke the device in Sheprd's remote control settings on the computer.

Get help

Open an issue on GitHub or ask on Discord. The docs cover Sheprd on the computer, and the privacy page says what the app shares.
